# Design Brief: NeuroSpark — Speech Switch Landing Page Clone

## Ground-truth Reference
- URL: `https://global-academia.com/mental/documentaryba/`
- Archetype: Direct-to-Consumer / VSL (Video Sales Letter) medical-educational style landing page for neuro-developmental program.
- Intent: Highly focused, low-distraction conversion structure designed around an attention-grabbing documentary headline, vertical video/demonstration area, media authority endorsement band, limited-time offer presentation, comprehensive FAQ accordion, risk reversal guarantee, and rigorous scientific reference documentation.

## Core Design Dimensions
1. **Design Movement**: Clean Direct-Response Medical/Educational VSL.
2. **Color Philosophy**:
   - Primary Dark Canvas: Deep Navy Blue (`#03051e` / `#050827`), providing maximum contrast for video content and headline authority.
   - Contrast Light Canvas: Pure White (`#ffffff`) for authority endorsement strip and scientific citations, maintaining medical/institutional credibility.
   - Brand Accent Red: Vivid Alert Red (`#e11d48` / `#dc2626` / `#e50914`), highlighting critical conversion triggers ("SPEECH SWITCH", "14 DAYS", countdown badges).
   - High-Conversion CTA Green: Energetic Vibrant Lime/Emerald Green (`#00c853` / `#16a34a` / `#22c55e`) with subtle top highlight and drop shadow for maximum tap affordance.
   - Text Palette: Pure white for hero titles, slate gray (`#94a3b8`) for secondary instructions, deep charcoal (`#1e293b`) for body paragraphs, and muted gray (`#64748b`) for reference footnotes.
3. **Typography System**:
   - Primary Headings: Bold condensed sans-serif (`Montserrat`, `Roboto`, or system-ui) in uppercase, high line-height control and letter-spacing for punchy reading.
   - Body & FAQ: Highly readable neutral sans-serif (`Inter` / `Roboto`) with comfortable 1.6 line height for dense explanatory paragraphs.
   - Scientific Citations: Compact academic sans-serif (`Open Sans` / system-ui, 12px, #888888) with numbered superscript alignment.
4. **Layout Paradigm**:
   - Single-column centered container (max 680px for hero video card, max 780px for sales copy and FAQ, max 1040px for references).
   - Fluid responsive gutters (16px mobile, 24px tablet, 32px desktop).
5. **Key Signature Elements**:
   - Hero text: `HOW TO ACTIVATE THE SPEECH SWITCH IN YOUR NON-VERBAL AUTISTIC CHILD IN UNDER 14 DAYS` with highlighted colored blocks.
   - Vertical Video Player Box with 4:5 / 9:16 aspect ratio, responsive bounds, audio overlay prompt ("Your video has started / Click to listen") and simulated progress bar.
   - Authority Media strip: clean white section displaying official publication marks.
   - Interactive Countdown: 3-block timer (Hours : Minutes : Seconds) formatted with clean digit cards and labels.
   - FAQ Accordion: Clean bordered cards with chevron indicators, smooth expand/collapse transitions, and full keyboard accessibility.
   - 100% Money Back Guarantee Badge: Gold circular icon badge with clear 60-day refund reassurance text.
   - Scientific Citations & Legal Disclaimer: Full compliance block with transparent academic source links and platform disclosures.
